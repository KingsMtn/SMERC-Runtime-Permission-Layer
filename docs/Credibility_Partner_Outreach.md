## What Not To Say

Do not claim:

- production certification
- customer validation
- incident reduction
- replacement for existing security platforms
- replacement for OPA, IAM, SOAR, SIEM, ServiceNow, Jira, AML, or model-risk systems
- guaranteed buyer demand

The right ask is credibility review and pilot-fit feedback.
